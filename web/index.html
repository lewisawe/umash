How Umash works

01

Immediate

Hours to days. Register the death, choose a mortuary against its clock, release the body, engage a funeral director, notify people.

02

Funeral

Days to a week. Venue, ceremony logistics, permits, the obituary, and coordinating contributions.

03

Admin & estate

Weeks to months. Banks, insurers, benefits, utilities, subscriptions, the employer, government, the estate.

04

Aftercare

Months to a year and beyond. Closing digital and social accounts, transferring the vehicle and property title, personal effects, a headstone, memorials, and grief support for the living.

The rule the product rests on

Routine, low-stakes work is prepared quietly and batched into one approval. Weighty, irreversible, or time-critical decisions are escalated one at a time, with the tradeoffs laid out. Umash never files, pays, or books anything on its own.
